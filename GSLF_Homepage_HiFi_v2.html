Placement

From enrolment to employment in 90 days.

A structured, support-intensive process that leaves nothing to chance — from the first aptitude test to the first salary slip.

  1. 01

    Assessment & Enrolment

    We run free aptitude and interest assessments at community level — in schools, ration shops, and gram panchayat offices. Every student who qualifies gets a scholarship covering 100% of tuition.

  2. 02

    Sector Training (45–60 days)

    Hands-on, employer-aligned training at one of 60 GSLF centres. Industry-certified trainers. Real tools. Soft-skills and English communication woven in throughout. NSQF-aligned certifications issued on completion.

  3. 03

    Placement Preparation (10 days)

    Resume building, mock interviews with real hiring managers, workplace etiquette, and financial literacy. We simulate the actual hiring process so students walk in confident.

  4. 04

    Job Placement & 6-Month Follow-up

    Direct placement with 100+ employer partners. Our placement cell stays in touch with every graduate — resolving disputes, providing career guidance, and tracking retention. 82% are still employed at the six-month mark.

How Employer Partnership Works
1

Submit a talent requirement

Share the roles, skills, location, and volume you need. We match it against current batches or schedule a dedicated cohort.

2

Co-design the training module

Our curriculum team works with your L&D leads to embed your tools, protocols, and SOPs directly into the training.

3

Interview & select at GSLF centres

Conduct campus-style drives at our training centres. Pre-screened graduates, structured interview slots, zero logistics overhead for your HR team.

4

Post-placement support

GSLF placement officers track every hire for six months, resolving early-stage friction before it becomes attrition.
