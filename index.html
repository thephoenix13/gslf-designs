GSLF — Design Concepts

Three high-fidelity homepage directions for gslfoundation.org — each tailored to a different positioning strategy. Select a design to preview in full.

Design V1  ·  Parchment Editorial
Gates Foundation / CZI — Data-Trust Enterprise →
56/44 split hero, warm parchment surface, borderless program panels, vertical numbered journey, maximum editorial whitespace.
Why this design works
  • Whitespace and restrained typography signal institutional credibility — exactly what CSR heads, government bodies, and large grant makers look for before they trust an NGO with funds.
  • The impact numbers sit raw on the page with no decorative tiles — a deliberate choice that reads as transparency, not marketing, which is critical for donor confidence.
  • The saffron CTA is used once per section, making every call-to-action feel intentional rather than pushy — ideal for an audience that dislikes being "sold to."
  • Parchment warmth softens the authority of the layout so it doesn't feel cold — appealing to both institutional funders and first-generation students visiting for the first time.
Best for: CSR teams · Institutional donors · Government partners · Policy audiences
Design V2  ·  Clean White Editorial
Full-Bleed Hero — Annual Report Precision →
Edge-to-edge full-width hero image with Ken Burns animation, horizontal program table rows, pure white surface, count-up impact numbers.
Why this design works
  • The full-bleed hero photograph commands attention immediately — real faces from real worksites create instant emotional resonance before a single word is read, which drives lower bounce rates.
  • The program table layout (sector · name · duration · salary · employers) answers every question a prospective student has in one scannable row — reducing friction between interest and application.
  • Clean white with precise grid lines communicates operational maturity — the kind of professionalism that makes corporates comfortable listing GSLF as a CSR partner in their own annual reports.
  • The count-up animation on impact numbers creates a moment of delight that subtly reinforces scale — 20,000+ trained feels more real when you watch the number build up live.
Best for: Prospective students · Urban middle-class parents · Corporate HR teams · Media & press
Design V3  ·  Human-First Contemporary
Image-Dominant Hero — Bold Warm Modern →
58% image-forward asymmetric hero, program cards with photo thumbnails, horizontal 4-step timeline, multi-direction slide-in animations.
Why this design works
  • Leading with a large, warm photograph of a real young person rather than a headline first is proven to drive higher empathy and time-on-page — the audience sees themselves (or their child) before they read anything.
  • Program cards with image thumbnails help semi-literate or first-generation users navigate intuitively — the visual cue of a photo next to "Refrigeration Technician" lowers cognitive load significantly.
  • The horizontal 4-step journey timeline makes the path from enrolment to employment feel short, clear, and achievable — directly addressing the hesitation of students who fear the process is complicated.
  • Slide-in animations and hover interactions give the site a premium, app-like feel that builds trust with younger audiences who judge credibility by how modern something looks.
Best for: First-gen students · Rural & semi-urban youth · Social media traffic · NGO award panels
