Our Understanding of the Brief

Based on BRD v2.1, June 2026 — competitive review of Pratham.org and QuestAlliance.net

GSLF is a skill-development nonprofit operating 400+ vocational centres across 12+ states, having trained over 20,000 students and placed 16,000+ in employment — an 80% placement rate that neither Pratham nor Quest Alliance can match. Despite this scale and outcome data, the current gslfoundation.org is a 2019-era brochure with placeholder content still live, missing active centres, and zero conversion architecture.

The BRD calls for a complete strategic repositioning: from an NGO information website to a Skill-to-Employment Platform. The new site must serve four distinct audiences — students (primary), employers (secondary), donors/CSR (tertiary), and industry partners — each with a dedicated journey, and must answer their primary question within 5 seconds of landing.

GSLF also has a for-profit arm, FGSPL (Funfirst Global Skills Pvt Ltd), that handles apprenticeship and employer-facing work. The redesign must present both entities in a legally clean, brand-consistent manner with appropriate disclosures, cross-linking, and a unified student UX across both properties.

The project has been benchmarked explicitly against Pratham.org and QuestAlliance.net. Our recommendation is to lead with GSLF's single biggest competitive advantage — placement outcomes with salary data — which neither competitor offers.

Key Assumptions

This estimate is valid under the following conditions
  • All content listed in BRD Section 10 (success stories, photos, course data, batch schedules, team bios, financials) is delivered by GSLF before development begins. No content = no go-live.
  • GSLF will provide a dedicated internal point of contact for weekly reviews and content approvals throughout Phase 1.
  • Hindi translations for student and course pages are provided by GSLF's internal team. We build the toggle infrastructure; GSLF provides the language content.
  • Zoho CRM account is active and accessible. GSLF's technology team handles Zoho configuration; we handle the website-side form integration.
  • WhatsApp Business API account is set up and verified by GSLF before WhatsApp handoff features go live.
  • Google Maps API key is provisioned by GSLF. API usage charges are billed directly to GSLF's account.
  • Estimate is based on BRD v2.1 scope. Any new requirements added post-sign-off will be quoted separately.
  • All prices are exclusive of GST (18%). Hosting, domain, and third-party software subscription costs are additional.
  • This estimate is valid for 30 days from the date of submission.
