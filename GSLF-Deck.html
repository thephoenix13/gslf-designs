About the client

Who they are.

Grey Sim Learnings Foundation (GSLF) is a Section 8 nonprofit that trains 20,000+ underprivileged youth annually — girls, PWD, and rural communities — in vocational trades like RACW, Optical Fiber, EV, and 5G across 30+ centres in 12+ states.

Founded in 2013, they've built a rare combination: CSR funding from HCL, HDFC, HSBC, and Tech Mahindra on one side — and direct hiring relationships with Voltas, LG, Whirlpool, and Panasonic on the other. An 80%+ placement rate backs it up.

GSLF has stronger employment outcomes than either Pratham or Quest Alliance — real salary data, real employers, real numbers. Their website just doesn't show any of it.

Current website — gap analysis · 1 of 2

Five gaps on the surface.

1
Impact counters show "0+"
The homepage displays animated counters that never load. Donors and partners see zero trained, zero placed, zero labs. A CSR manager leaves in under 10 seconds.
2
The enrollment form doesn't capture leads
Phone number is not required. No state/city field. No education qualification. No attribution. No confirmation. It collects names — not leads. Zoho receives nothing actionable.
3
Zero employer-facing content
No employer landing page. No hiring process. No apprenticeship details. No Request Talent form. GSLF's biggest differentiator vs Pratham and Quest Alliance doesn't exist on the site.
4
Course pages have no conversion data
No expected salary. No batch dates. No hiring company logos. No career progression pathways. A student looking at RACW Technician has no reason to enroll over any alternative.
5
WhatsApp — the actual conversion channel — doesn't exist on the site
Every enrolled student likely came through WhatsApp. The website has no WhatsApp button, no counselor link, no deeplink. The top-of-funnel and the conversion channel are completely disconnected.

Current website — gap analysis · 2 of 2

Five more. Deeper and costlier.

6
Success stories have no salary data or photos
Three text-only case studies from 2023. No photos, no before/after salary, no employer named prominently. Pratham's version is stronger — and GSLF's stories are actually better.
7
About Us earns zero donor confidence
No audited financials. No Annual Report. No board governance section. No MoU list. Pratham publishes 10 years of financials. GSLF publishes certificates in a dropdown.
8
News section actively damages credibility
Three placeholder blog posts from 2019 — visible to every visitor. Copyright footer reads 2023. The signal: nobody is looking after this site.
9
GSLF and FGSPL are invisible to each other
FGSPL (funfirst.in) runs the same mission, same courses, same locations — with zero cross-linking to GSLF. Donors researching GSLF can't find the commercial arm. Students can't navigate between them.
10
Hindi, interactive map, and SEO pages all missing
Primary student audience is Hindi-speaking. Center locator is a text list. No city-level SEO pages for "EV Technician Course Pune" or "Free Training Chennai." Organic traffic is near zero.

The current site is underperforming at every stage — discovery (no SEO), evaluation (no salary data, no proof), and conversion (broken form, no WhatsApp). This is not a cosmetic redesign.

Discovery · What we'll bring to the room

The questions that unlock this project.

Strategy & Architecture
01
Who's actually visiting the site today?
We need Google Analytics access — visitor breakdown before designing for 4 audiences.
02
What is WITP — walk us through the student journey.
Your biggest differentiator vs Pratham and Quest. We need to understand it to design the right pages.
03
Where does GSLF end and FGSPL begin for a student?
funfirst.in and gslfoundation.org have zero cross-linking today. What's the intended student journey across both?
04
Is FGSPL's website also being redesigned — same vendor or different?
The BRD requires consistent UX across both sites. This is only achievable with a design system handoff or a single vendor.
05
FGSPL is blue/teal. Does GSLF inherit this or go its own direction?
Color system decision determines whether the "consistent UX" requirement is achievable from day one of design.
06
Is there a go-live target and a budget range?
The BRD has no dates and no budget. We need both to scope correctly and protect your timeline.
Content, Systems & Readiness
07
Are any of the 5 success stories already written?
Lubna is named as owner. Do students have signed photo consent? This is the longest lead-time content item.
08
Where do impact numbers live — spreadsheet, Zoho, or a manual count?
Shani owns the update. We need to know the source system to design the right admin workflow.
09
When will the Annual Report FY25-26 be ready?
Listed as TBD by Operations + Finance. If this isn't ready, the Donor section launches with a placeholder — not empty.
10
Is Zoho CRM live and receiving leads today, or is this new?
Every lead form on the new site flows to Zoho. If the CRM isn't configured, we deliver endpoints that go nowhere.
11
Is the Devjit's team resourced for Zoho + WhatsApp API setup?
Both are out of vendor scope. If they're not set up before go-live testing, chatbot leads vanish and WhatsApp doesn't fire.
12
Do Center HODs submit batch schedules anywhere today?
The BRD requires batch dates per center, pan-India. If there's no existing process, this needs to be designed before development begins.
