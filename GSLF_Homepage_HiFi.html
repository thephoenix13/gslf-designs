The GSLF Pathway

From enrolment to employment — in 90 days.

Apply for the next batch →
01
Enroll
Choose a course that matches your interest and location. Apply online or walk into your nearest GSLF centre — no prior qualifications required for most programs.
02
Learn in a Real Lab
3–4 months of hands-on training led by certified industry trainers. No passive lectures. Every session is structured around the tools, machines, and scenarios you will face on the job.
03
Get Certified
Earn your NCVET or NSDC certification — a credential recognised by 100+ employers nationally and respected by state governments and public sector undertakings.
04
Get Placed
Our placement cell connects you to verified employer partners. Attend job fairs, campus interviews, or direct referrals. We stay with you until you have a confirmed offer and a joining date.
